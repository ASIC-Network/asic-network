About the network.

01What it is

Chip design is hard to break into and easy to do alone. We make it a team sport.

01

Documentation

Open, shared docs. Tooling notes, design flows, and guides any member club can use and contribute to.

02

Biweekly updates

Every two weeks clubs share progress and blockers. A simple rhythm to keep things moving and learn from each other.

03

Open on GitHub

Everything lives in the open. Fork the repos, file issues, send pull requests.

04

Community

A shared Discord where clubs talk day to day. Register your club to get an invite.

Independent student groups, working in the open and sharing what we learn. No gatekeeping, no silos. Just one signal across clubs building together.

Founded by Avyukt Raghuvanshi at the University of Minnesota, where Stone Arch Silicon is the founding chapter.
